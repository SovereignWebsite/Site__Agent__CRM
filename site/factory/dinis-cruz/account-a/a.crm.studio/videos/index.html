CRM explainer videos

Short videos of the CRM tool, made from its demo mode: every company, person and email in them is made up. Each is captured by a script with headless Chromium, the clock pinned and only the CRM's own site reachable, so the same script on the same site version gives the same video. The written guide: How to use the CRM.

The CRM, and its demo

64 seconds · 12 scenes · captured from https://crm.tool.sovereignweb.site v0.1.9 · 548ae6e · 2026-11-24 with the clock at 2026-11-24T10:00:00Z

Script (the captions, in order)
  1. The CRM keeps a small team's companies, people and actions in one place.
  2. It runs in your browser, and opens in demo mode. This banner says every company, person and email here is made up.
  3. The top right always says which vault is open. Here: Vault: Demo. Nothing you do in the demo is saved anywhere.
  4. Today opens on how many companies sit at each stage, from New to Won and Parked.
  5. Then what to do now. Overdue comes first: here, a next step that was due on 21 November.
  6. Next, follow-ups: we wrote and have had no reply for 5 days or more.
  7. Each card has its action. Log the follow-up records what you did next.
  8. The lead board: one row per company, with its stage, owner, draft, last contact and next step.
  9. Follow-ups: everyone we reached out to who has not replied in 5 days or more. Check it every day.
  10. Contacts: everyone, with their company and when we were last in touch. Demo emails use reserved example domains.
  11. Open a company to see its stage, owner, next step, people and every action logged with them.
  12. That is the demo. Next: logging in and opening a vault.

Today, and logging an action

68 seconds · 13 scenes · captured from https://crm.tool.sovereignweb.site v0.1.9 · 548ae6e · 2026-11-24 with the clock at 2026-11-24T10:00:00Z

Script (the captions, in order)
  1. The daily loop: see what is due, do it, log it, set the next step.
  2. Start every day on Today. It lists what to do now, each with its action.
  3. First, overdue. Example Ventures asked questions on our proposal, and the answer was due on 21 November.
  4. Say we have answered them by email. Press Log the follow-up on its card.
  5. The company's page opens on Log an action: what, which way, when, who, and one line on what happened.
  6. Here: an email, we reached out, on 23 November, to Diogo Quinn, answering their questions.
  7. Show what will be written shows the exact file first. In the demo it stays in this tab: nothing is written anywhere.
  8. Press Write it. The action is now first in the company's Activity. In a real vault, it is one change you can see in its history.
  9. Now the next step. Edit stage, owner and next step keeps the lead row up to date.
  10. The next step becomes: wait for their decision, due 30 November.
  11. Show what will be written, then Write it. The company now shows the new next step.
  12. Back on Today, Example Ventures is no longer overdue. Do this for each card, every day.
  13. Next: follow-ups.
