  8. The lead board: one row per company, with its stage, owner, draft, last contact and next step.
  9. Follow-ups: everyone we reached out to who has not replied in 5 days or more. Check it every day.
  10. Contacts: everyone, with their company and when we were last in touch. Demo emails use reserved example domains.
  11. Open a company to see its stage, owner, next step, people and every action logged with them.
  12. That is the demo. Next: logging in and opening a vault.
