# Plan: "Dinis builds it", narrated cuts of the CRM explainers

Status: a plan, waiting for footage. Written 25 Nov 2026 by a.crm.studio, from Dinis's direction as relayed by a.maestro (01:43 UTC, close to verbatim): "create videos where me and other humans, fundamentally, are narrating, where we actually are implementing ... use some of the footage that I recorded ... add the explanation on top ... brings everything to life, when it's not just those animated agents ... they can turn up in animations." Not yet confirmed by Dinis in this session.

## The format
One cut per explainer topic, in the same player, mixing three kinds of scene:

1. **Footage**: Dinis (or another person who has consented) at work, with his own voice. A segment is a source, an in point and an out point; nothing is re-encoded unless blurring needs it.
2. **Captures**: the existing demo-mode screens, with the amber frame, for the step he is talking about (deterministic, as now).
3. **Overlays on top of footage**: a caption line, a step chip and, where it helps, the Studio presenter in the corner pointing at what matters. The agents "turn up in animations"; the person stays the narrator.

When footage plays, its own audio is the narration and the browser's speech is off. On capture scenes the speech reads the caption, as now, unless Dinis records a line for it.

## How it fits the player
- `scenes.json` gains a scene type `footage`: `{ "footage": { "src": "<url>", "in": 12.4, "out": 31.0 }, "caption": "...", "overlay": { ... } }`.
- The player plays that range in a `<video>` element on the white stage, with captions and overlays drawn over it as HTML, then moves to the next scene. A capture scene can follow a footage scene directly ("this is what he just did, in the demo").
- The script stays the source: which segment, which caption, which capture. The page is still generated by `studio/page.mjs`.

## Where footage lives (never in this repository until Dinis approves)
- Raw footage stays where Dinis keeps it. Working copies live only in this session's scratchpad.
- An approved, blurred cut goes to a **public, read-only sgit vault** (or a store Dinis names), and the page loads it from there. This repository holds only the script, the scenes and the screens, as now. That also keeps the repository small (his earlier point about video size).
- Before Dinis approves, a cut is shown to him only (a private artifact or a vault he alone holds), never published.

## Privacy, before anything is shown to anyone
- **Other people** appear only with their written consent, recorded in this area (`consents.md`: who, what, when, how given). No consent: cut them out or blur them beyond recognition.
- **Private screen data** (real contacts, emails, keys, tokens, vault ids, other windows, notifications) is blurred. I list every region per segment (time range and box) in the script, blur it with ffmpeg in the scratchpad, and check every frame range by eye before Dinis sees it.
- Real CRM data never appears: where his footage shows the live CRM with real contacts, that stretch is replaced by the demo-mode capture of the same step.

## Who does what
- a.crm.studio: finds the segments, writes the script, blurs, builds the cut, checks it, and shows it to Dinis.
- Dinis: provides the footage, approves each cut, and posts or says where it goes. Agents prepare, people post.
- a.instagram has the same direction for its posts; we share segments and blur lists where they overlap, not footage outside approved stores.
